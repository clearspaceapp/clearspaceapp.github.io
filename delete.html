Delete your ClearSpace data

App: ClearSpace – Photo Cleanup · Developer: ClearSpace (mobirexa@gmail.com)

ClearSpace has no accounts. Your photos and videos never leave your device. The only data stored outside your device is a purchase record kept by our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

Data on your device

Analysis results, review decisions and settings are stored only in the app's private storage. To delete them, uninstall ClearSpace or open Android Settings → Apps → ClearSpace → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes them immediately.

Purchase record (RevenueCat)

To ask us to delete your purchase record:

  1. Email mobirexa@gmail.com with the subject "ClearSpace data deletion".
  2. Include your Google Play order number (starts with "GPA.", found in the purchase email from Google or under Payments & subscriptions in the Play Store), so we can find the record. If you never bought ClearSpace Pro, no record exists.

We delete the record within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: the anonymous app user ID and the purchase and subscription history linked to it at RevenueCat.
  • Not affected: your subscription itself. Cancel it in Google Play first if you no longer want it; deleting the record does not cancel or refund it.
  • Kept by Google: payment and order records that Google Play must keep under its own policies and the law. Those are handled by Google, not by us.
