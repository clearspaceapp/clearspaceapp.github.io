ClearSpace Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · Applies to ClearSpace for Android and iOS

ClearSpace helps you review and tidy the photos and videos on your device. It is built so that your media never leaves your phone. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it stores, and the few cases where data is shared with a service provider.

Your photos and videos

  • ClearSpace reads the photos and videos you allow it to access, so it can show categories, find similar and duplicate photos, detect blurry photos and compress videos.
  • All analysis happens on your device. Your photos, videos, their contents and their location data are never uploaded, sold or shared.
  • To confirm that two files are exact copies, the app may read the original file, which can include embedded location (EXIF) data. This data is only compared on the device and is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Analysis results (for example image fingerprints, sharpness scores and your review decisions) are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • ClearSpace only moves items to the trash or deletes them after you review and confirm the selection. On Android, the system also asks for your confirmation unless you have allowed ClearSpace to manage media.

Purchases and subscriptions

ClearSpace Pro is sold through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Payments are processed by Google or Apple; ClearSpace never receives your card or payment details.

To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the app, your purchase and subscription history from the store, and basic technical information such as the app version and device type. This data is used only to provide and restore your purchases. It is not linked to your photos.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, ClearSpace schedules a local reminder on your device before the trial ends. No notification data is sent to a server.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers or ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no selling of personal data.

Children

ClearSpace is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • You can change or revoke photo and video access at any time in your device settings.
  • You can exclude folders from scanning in ClearSpace's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device. To ask for RevenueCat-held purchase records to be deleted, follow the steps on the data deletion page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
