ClearSpace Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · Applies to ClearSpace for iPhone (iOS)

ClearSpace helps you review and tidy the photos and videos on your iPhone. It is built so that your media never leaves your device. The iOS app does not collect any personal data, and it does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Your photos and videos

  • ClearSpace reads the photos and videos you allow it to access in Photos, so it can show categories, find similar photos and exact copies, suggest blurry photos and create smaller copies of videos. You can allow full or limited access.
  • All analysis happens on your iPhone. Your photos, videos, their contents and their location data are never uploaded, sold or shared.
  • To confirm that two files are exact copies, the app reads the original files stored on your iPhone, which can include embedded location (EXIF) data. The contents are only compared on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere. Items stored only in iCloud are skipped; the app does not download them.
  • Analysis results (for example image fingerprints, sharpness scores, file sizes and your review decisions) are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone and are excluded from backups. They are removed when you uninstall the app. Turning off photo access also removes the saved scan results.
  • ClearSpace only deletes items after you review and confirm the selection, and iOS asks for your confirmation again. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in Photos. Favorites are protected and cannot be deleted from the app.

Purchases and subscriptions

ClearSpace Pro is sold through the App Store. Payments are processed by Apple; ClearSpace never receives your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks your subscription status directly with Apple on your device. No subscription service provider or server of ours is involved.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers or ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No collection, sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

ClearSpace is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • You can change or revoke photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → ClearSpace.
  • Uninstalling ClearSpace removes all data it stored on your iPhone.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
