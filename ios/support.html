I deleted photos but my storage did not change

Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in Photos and stay there for 30 days, so you can change your mind. To free the space now, open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted and delete them there. With iCloud Photos, storage on other devices can take a while to update.

Some photos are missing from the results

  • If you chose limited access, only the photos you selected are scanned. To change it: Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → ClearSpace → Full Access.
  • Photos and videos stored only in iCloud are skipped. Download them in Photos (or turn on “Download and Keep Originals”) and scan again.
  • Very dark or plain photos may be left out of similarity suggestions because they cannot be compared reliably.

Why can't I delete a photo?

Favorites are protected. Remove a photo from Favorites in Photos to delete it in ClearSpace. In exact copies, at least one copy in each group always stays.

Free plan and ClearSpace Pro

Scanning, previews and reviewing are free. The free plan includes 20 deletions and 1 compressed video save per day; the allowance resets at local midnight. ClearSpace Pro removes these daily limits.

Restore or manage a subscription

  • To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open the plans screen in ClearSpace and tap Restore, using the same Apple Account.
  • To cancel or change your plan: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ClearSpace. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
  • For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Your data

ClearSpace has no accounts and does not collect personal data. All analysis happens on your iPhone. Read the Privacy Policy.
