Keep the photos you love. Clear the rest.

ClearSpace finds similar shots, exact copies, blurry photos and large videos, so you can decide what goes. Everything happens on your phone.

Coming soon to Android

It sorts your library into similar photos , exact copies , screenshots, blurry shots , selfies, Live Photos and the large videos that quietly fill your storage.

Three steps, and you decide each one

  1. Scan

    ClearSpace reads your library on the device and groups it into categories. The next time you open the app, only what changed is checked.

  2. Review

    Similar shots come with a suggested keeper and the reason for it. Favorites are protected and can never be selected.

  3. Confirm

    Nothing is deleted until you confirm. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted first, so you have time to change your mind.

A few more ways to tidy up

Swipe through your photos
Right to keep, left to set aside. A small daily goal keeps it light, and your progress is saved.
Exact copies, checked properly
A separate check compares the actual file contents, so only true copies are grouped. One copy always stays.
Smaller videos
Save a compressed copy of a large video, compare both, and keep the original if you prefer.
Widgets
See today's progress and your library at a glance from the Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Questions

Why didn't my storage change after deleting?

Deleted items move to Recently Deleted (on Android, the trash) and stay there for 30 days. Empty it in your photo app to free the space right away.

Does ClearSpace clear system data or other apps?

No. It organizes your photo and video library. It doesn't touch system storage, caches or other apps.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Scanning, previews and reviewing are free, with a daily allowance of deletions and compressed video saves. ClearSpace Pro removes the daily limits. Prices and any free trial are shown in the app before you buy.

Why are some photos missing from the results?

Photos stored only in iCloud or the cloud are skipped until they're on the device. If you allowed access to selected photos only, just those are scanned.

Is it available on Android?

ClearSpace is on the App Store for iPhone. The Android version is coming soon to Google Play.
